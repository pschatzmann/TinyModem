Detailed Description

Configuration of the AX.25 packet radio modem (AX25Encoder, AX25Decoder): Bell 202 AFSK with 1200 baud, NRZI and HDLC framing, as used by APRS.

setMode() selects "ax25" or "aprs" (standard AX.25), "fx25" (FX.25 with 16 check bytes), "fx25-32", "fx25-64" or "il2p" (IL2P with max FEC) for the transmitter. The receiver decodes all of them (like direwolf).
