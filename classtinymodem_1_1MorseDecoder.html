Detailed Description

Morse code demodulator (CW): the PCM audio data is written to the decoder and the decoded characters (upper case) are written to the output. The input can be 8, 16 or 32 bit signed samples with any number of channels (only MorseConfig::channel is evaluated).

The level of the tone is measured with a mixer and a sliding sum over half a dot, every 2 ms. The tone is on when the level is above the middle between the signal and the noise level (with hysteresis), and short changes are ignored. The length of the marks and spaces in units provides the dots and dashes and the spaces between the elements, characters and words. The length of a unit adapts to the speed of the sender. The rise and fall time of the tone shortens the marks and lengthens the spaces by the same amount: this is measured with the dots and the following element spaces (both are 1 unit) and corrected.

A transmission ends after MorseConfig::rx_timeout_units without a tone: this is reported with ### NOCARRIER.
