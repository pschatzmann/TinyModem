Detailed Description

Configuration of the PSK modem (PSKEncoder, PSKDecoder): differential PSK with cosine shaped transitions like PSK31, compatible with fldigi.

setMode() selects "psk31", "psk63", "psk125", "psk250" or "psk500" (BPSK, 31.25 to 500 baud), "qpsk31" to "qpsk500" (QPSK with a K=5 convolutional code, same speed with FEC) or "8psk125", "8psk250", "8psk500" and "8psk1000" (8PSK without FEC, 3 bits per symbol). The carrier frequency is 1000 Hz by default: 8psk500 and 8psk1000 raise it to twice the symbol rate.
