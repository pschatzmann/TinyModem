Detailed Description

Configuration of the Olivia and Contestia modems (OliviaEncoder, OliviaDecoder): MFSK with 2 to 256 tones and a strong forward error correction (Walsh-Hadamard codes over blocks of 64 or 32 symbols), as designed by Pawel Jalocha and implemented by fldigi.

setMode() selects "olivia-T-B" or "contestia-T-B" with T tones and a bandwidth B of 125, 250, 500, 1000 or 2000 Hz (also written as 1k or 2k), e.g. "olivia-8-250" or "olivia-32-1000". "olivia" is "olivia-32-1000", "contestia" is "contestia-8-250". Olivia sends 7 bit characters (and 8 bit characters escaped with 127 like fldigi), Contestia sends upper case text with 6 bit characters at twice the speed: a line break is sent as '\r' (which fldigi shows as new line), '\r' is ignored.

The signal is defined at 8000 Hz: other sample rates are converted.
