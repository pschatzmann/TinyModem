Detailed Description

Configuration of the DTMF (touch tone) modem (DTMFEncoder, DTMFDecoder): each of the characters 0-9, *, #, A, B, C and D is sent as the sum of a row tone (697 - 941 Hz) and a column tone (1209 - 1633 Hz).

The receiver needs tones and pauses of at least about 45 ms. The mode name is "dtmf".
