Detailed Description

DTMF (touch tone) modulator: each written character 0-9, *, #, A-D (also a-d) is sent as a tone pair of DTMFConfig::tone_ms followed by a pause of DTMFConfig::pause_ms. A ',' adds a pause of DTMFConfig::comma_ms, all other characters are ignored. The tones fade in and out within 2 ms to avoid clicks.
