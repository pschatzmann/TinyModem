Detailed Description

AX.25 packet radio demodulator (APRS, 1200 baud AFSK). The PCM audio data is written to the decoder and the received frames with a correct frame check sequence are written to the output: as monitor text lines (SRC>DEST,DIGI:info) or as KISS frames (see AX25Config::format).

The mark and space energy is measured with sliding correlators over one bit, a digital PLL recovers the bit clock, and the bits are NRZI and HDLC decoded. The input can be 8, 16 or 32 bit signed samples with any number of channels (only AX25Config::channel is evaluated).

FX.25 and IL2P frames are decoded in parallel (independent of AX25Config::layer2) and corrected with their Reed-Solomon codes. An FX.25 frame which is also received as standard AX.25 frame is provided only once.
