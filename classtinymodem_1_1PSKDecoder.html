Detailed Description

PSK demodulator (BPSK, QPSK and 8PSK compatible with fldigi): the PCM audio data is written to the decoder and the decoded bytes are written to the output. The input can be 8, 16 or 32 bit signed samples with any number of channels (only PSKConfig::channel is evaluated).

The signal is mixed to the baseband and integrated over one symbol. The symbol timing is determined from the energy distribution over the symbol period, the bits from the phase difference of consecutive symbols: QPSK uses a soft decision Viterbi decoder. An automatic frequency control tracks frequency offsets of up to a quarter of the symbol rate (QPSK: 1/8, 8PSK: 1/16). The receiver starts with a run of phase reversals (preamble) and stops when the signal is lost or with a long steady carrier (postamble of BPSK and 8PSK).
