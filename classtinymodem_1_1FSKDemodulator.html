Detailed Description

FSK bit and frame analysis (port of minimodem's fsk.c). Instead of a full FFT we use the Goertzel algorithm from the AudioTools to determine the magnitude of the mark and space bands. The bands are defined like the FFT bins of minimodem, so the results are equivalent.
